# Omnii

A personal background helper for Alex: a loop that looks at what is on their plate every few minutes, asks one-sentence questions with fixed options, keeps rhythms (medicine, apartment), watches a folder and mail, and never sends anything in their name. Everything it knows is a plain file under `desk/`.

## Who uses it

Alex alone. Not a developer. Reads fast, dislikes being made to read twice, and will not micromanage an interface. Uses it on a Mac in a dark room, and from the phone over iMessage.

## Surfaces

- **Desk** (Operate): what needs attention right now, and the state of everything else at a glance. Act here.
- **Config** (Operate): the loop's rulebook. One row per rule, every choice visible, change it in place. Set here.
- **History** (Read): what Omnii saw, did and left alone, each look.
- **Threads** (Operate): conversation with a mode.

## Voice

Omnii speaks of itself as Omnii, in plain everyday words. Labels are nouns. Rules read as facts, not instructions.

## Non-goals

Dashboards, charts, onboarding tours, marketing. Nothing here persuades; it informs and lets Alex set things.
